Little by little, history connects.

A small habit for a bigger goal.

Original world-history practice for learners who want to understand the past. This edition has 5,000 learning entries across 40 sections, alongside the original 320 questions. There are 150 original explanations and 4,850 source-based cards assembled from public Wikidata records; the latter have not been individually reviewed. This is not a university past paper or an admissions prediction.

Practise terms, dates and historical connections in short rounds. Mistakes remain in your review list until you answer correctly. XP measures practice, not proficiency or admission chances.

This edition does not cover every part of History Overview, map analysis, source interpretation or essay writing. Use it with textbooks, reference materials and past papers for your chosen faculty. Exam routes and subjects vary by faculty and year.

Content and references

The original questions, answers and explanations were reviewed on 3 November 2026. Source-based cards link to their source records. Dates may be approximate or disputed; reference links appear after answers. Historical interpretations can differ. English names and descriptions for source-based cards are localized from the same Wikidata entities; date precision and question IDs are preserved.

The dachshund tutor is an original study mascot. Decorative illustrations are not historical evidence.
